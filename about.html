About CANASA

The National Home of Speaking in Cameroon

Who we are, who leads us, and where we are going.

Who We Are

The Cameroon National Speakers Association (CANASA) is the national professional home for the speaking industry in Cameroon. CANASA exists to develop world-class Cameroonian speakers, leaders, and entrepreneurs — and the institutions that sustain them.

Our Mission

To build a national ecosystem for leadership development, public speaking, professional training, youth empowerment, and entrepreneurship — through education, mentorship, and international collaboration.

Founder & Technical Advisor

Oliver Nshom — professional speaker of 17+ years and founder of Tolkz Global, Oliver has spoken on stages across Africa, the United States, Europe and Asia, and coaches emerging leaders across Africa and beyond. He founded CANASA to give Cameroonian speakers what he had to build the hard way: a professional home, a standard to grow toward, and a stage.

National Leadership

Oliver Nshom

Founder & Technical Advisor

READ HIS SHOWCASE

Oliver Nshom is a professional speaker of more than seventeen years whose voice has carried from Cameroon to stages across sixteen countries, including Singapore, the United States, Spain and India. After more than a decade of speaking without the recognition his gift deserved, his 2023 breakthrough confirmed the conviction that built CANASA: Cameroonian speakers do not lack talent — they lack a professional home, a standard to grow toward, and a stage.

He is the Founder and CEO of Tolkz Speakers USA and the Founder of the Texas Institute of Public Speaking (TIPS), through which he coaches and certifies speakers internationally. He is the author of Beyond the Degree, Speaking Across Cultures, Love Across the Miles and Speak with Impact, and serves as a mentor to a rising generation of African speakers at home and in the diaspora.

As Founder and Technical Advisor of CANASA, he sets the Association’s standard and direction — its certification pathway, its national convention, and its Young Voices Academy for the next generation. His place at the head of this leadership is not a title of honour; it is the working office of the man who built the house and still holds its blueprint.
