CANASA/TIPS Professional Speakers Graduation 2027

When: Dates to Be Announced  ·  Where: Venue To Be Announced

Theme: Celebrating Excellence in Professional Communication

The CANASA/TIPS Professional Speakers Graduation celebrates individuals who have successfully completed structured training and professional development programs in public speaking, leadership communication, and presentation mastery.

Organized in collaboration with the Texas Institute of Public Speaking (TIPS), this event recognizes the dedication and achievements of individuals committed to developing professional communication skills.

Program Highlights

  • Graduation and certificate presentation
  • Outstanding graduate recognition
  • Graduate speaking showcases
  • Professional networking
  • Mentorship and career development opportunities

Target Audience: Public speaking trainees, emerging speakers, communication professionals, coaches, and educators.
