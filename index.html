Our Programmes

From children taking their first stage to professionals earning certification — CANASA builds speakers at every level.

Ages 7–17

Global Voices Academy

Public-speaking training for children and teenagers — Saturday academies, holiday camps and school clubs, showcased on Thrive TV.

Professional Standard

CANASA Certification

CANASA's own competency-based speaker certification, run in partnership with the Texas Institute of Public Speaking (TIPS) — examined, not merely attended.

A multi-purpose creative and media complex in Cameroon — studios, training and events under one roof.
