Membership

Belong to the Profession

CANASA membership puts you inside Cameroon’s speaking profession — its standard, its stages and its community.

Why Join

A Professional Home

Belong to the national body for speakers in Cameroon — be counted among the profession, not outside it.

Training & Certification

Grow through chapter trainings and the TIPS certification pathway — a standard that is examined, not merely attended.

Stages & Visibility

Access CANASA stages — chapter events, demo classes and the National Speakers Convention each December.

A Working Network

Five chapters of speakers, trainers and communication professionals who share opportunities and sharpen one another.

Who It’s For

  • Professional and aspiring public speakers
  • Trainers, coaches and facilitators
  • MCs, presenters and media communicators
  • Leaders whose work depends on the spoken word

How to Join

Register your interest today and the national secretariat will walk you through membership. You can also join in person at the National Speakers Convention on December 5, 2026 in Buea, where the membership desk will be open all day.
