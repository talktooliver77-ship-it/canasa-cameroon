Global Voices Academy

CANASA’s public-speaking programme for children and teenagers aged 7–17: Saturday academies, holiday camps and licensed school clubs, built on the TIPS curriculum and showcased on Thrive TV. The Academy is announced on stage at the National Speakers Convention on December 5, 2026.

  • Free demo classes: December 13 & 20, 2026 — Bafoussam, Bamenda, Douala, Yaoundé and Buea
  • Reservations: talktooliver77@gmail.com · WhatsApp +1 507-319-5381
  • Schools can partner to host clubs and inter-school speaking competitions

TIPS Certification

Certification under licence from the Texas Institute of Public Speaking (TIPS), administered in Cameroon by CANASA. Certification is competency-based: candidates train, are examined, and earn the standard — completion alone is not accreditation.

The Ten

Premium Certification Training

Three weeks of professional training for ten candidates. 100,000 FCFA in-person · 60,000 FCFA virtual.

The First Seven

Certification Examination

The first TIPS Certification Examination in Cameroon — seven candidates, certificates 001–007, awarded on stage December 5. 25,000 FCFA (TIPS graduates) · 50,000 FCFA direct entry.

  • Applications close November 7, 2026 · Selections November 10 · Training November 16 – December 2 · Examination December 3, Buea
  • A 50% deposit claims your seat once selected
