How Voting Will Work

  • One verified vote per person, per award category.
  • Voting is free — no purchase is ever required.
  • Voters are verified by email or phone, with anti-bot and duplicate-vote controls.
  • Public rankings are hidden and frozen before the awards ceremony — no live leaderboard.
  • Final results are published only after verification (December 1–3, 2026).

How Winners Are Decided

40%Public Vote

Supporters across Cameroon and the world vote for their finalists.

60%Independent Judges

A panel of independent judges scores finalists against documented criteria.
